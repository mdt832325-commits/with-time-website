The Future

Tomorrow's Yesterday

Everything you are waiting for will one day be something you remember. The future is just yesterday, still on its way.

With Time · November 9, 2026 · 4 min read

Everything you are waiting for will one day be something you remember. The exam. The wedding. The baby. The move. The phone call with the news. Right now they are all future, bright and uncertain. But one day you will sit somewhere quiet and say, 'Do you remember when we were waiting for that?' — and it will sound like a story about someone else.

That is the strange trick time plays on us. The future feels enormous when it is ahead of you, and small when it is behind you. The year you thought would never end is now a sentence you tell at dinner. The waiting that felt endless was, in the end, only a season. And the things you waited for were rarely the things that changed you. It was the waiting that did.

Think of the people you know who are waiting now: for a visa, for a result, for a loved one to come home, for a door to open. If you could tell them one thing, it would be this — the waiting is not wasted. It is doing its quiet work, teaching you patience, teaching you what matters, teaching you to notice the small good days that happen in between. The future arrives, but the waiting is where you live.

So if you are waiting tonight, be gentle with yourself. Make tea. Look out of the window. Call someone you miss. The thing you are waiting for is on its way, as surely as morning. And when it comes, it will become a yesterday — and you will be surprised at how fondly you remember even this, the waiting itself.

Tomorrow's yesterday is being written right now, by you, in this ordinary hour. Write it kindly.

“Do not be in such a hurry to arrive. The waiting is not the delay. The waiting is the life.”
Your turn

What are you waiting for right now — and how will you remember this waiting, ten years from today?
